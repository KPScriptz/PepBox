Modifications by PivotXP, starting 2026-09-27:
- Renamed the app, bundle identifier (com.pivotxp.PepBox), URL scheme
  (pepbox://), helper tools and all user-facing text from Droppy to PepBox,
  per the Droppy trademark policy (see TRADEMARK).
- Replaced the app icon, menu bar icon and Alfred workflow icon.
- Removed the upstream analytics and extension-ratings service; PepBox sends
  no usage data.
- Bundled extension icons and screenshots in the app (PepBox/ExtensionMedia)
  instead of loading them from the Droppy website.
- Pointed the update checker, crash-report links, website, Homebrew cask and
  release script at KPScriptz/PepBox; the release script takes the signing
  identity and team from the environment and no longer expects Gumroad keys.
- Replaced Droppy's logos on the website; its stats backend is disabled.
- Kept Droppy's original README, release notes and launch post, unmodified,
  in upstream/.
