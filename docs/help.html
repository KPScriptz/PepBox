FAQ

Is PepBox really free?

Yes. No accounts, no trials and no paid tier. The full source is on GitHub under the GPL-3.0 with the Commons Clause; see open-source licenses.

Does my Mac need a notch?

No. On Macs without a notch, and on external displays, PepBox shows a small floating pill at the top of the screen. You can also hide it on external displays in Settings.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

How do I open the shelf?

Drag a file toward the notch, or hover over the notch. Drop files on it to keep them there until you need them.

How do I turn on extensions?

Open PepBox's settings and go to Extensions. Each one can be turned on or off on its own. See the full list.

How do I update?

PepBox checks GitHub for new releases and lets you know. You can always grab the newest version from the latest release.

How do I uninstall?

Quit PepBox from its menu bar icon, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. To clear its settings first, use Settings → About → Hard Reset.

My shortcuts stopped working.

Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. If PepBox is listed but shortcuts still fail, toggle it off and on. More fixes are on the download page.

Privacy

PepBox has no analytics and no tracking. It does not send usage data anywhere. Your clipboard history, notes and settings stay on your Mac.

A few features need the internet. Each one only connects when you use it:

ServiceWhen
Open-MeteoWeather in Up Next and Quick Search
LRCLIBSynced lyrics, only if you turn lyrics on
open.er-api.comDaily currency rates for Quick Search conversions
api.ipify.orgThe ip command in Quick Search
0x0.stQuickshare and To Phone uploads; the file becomes reachable by its link
GitHubChecking for new PepBox releases

LocalSend stays on your local network. Files go straight from your Mac to the other device over your Wi-Fi, with no server in between.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Include your macOS version and what you were doing when it happened.
