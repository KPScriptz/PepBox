Changes made in PepBox

Modifications by PivotXP, starting 2026-09-27:

  • Renamed the app, bundle identifier (com.pivotxp.PepBox), URL scheme (pepbox://), helper tools and all user-facing text from Droppy to PepBox, per the Droppy trademark policy.
  • Replaced the app icon, menu bar icon and Alfred workflow icon.
  • Removed the upstream analytics and extension-ratings service; PepBox sends no usage data.
  • Bundled extension icons and screenshots in the app instead of loading them from the Droppy website.
  • Pointed the update checker, crash-report links, website, Homebrew cask and release script at KPScriptz/PepBox; the release script takes the signing identity and team from the environment and no longer expects Gumroad keys.
  • Replaced Droppy's logos on the website; its stats backend is disabled.
  • Kept Droppy's original README, release notes and launch post, unmodified, in upstream/.

Text Actions

Text Actions (Packages/TextActionsKit) is DropClip, based on OpenClip by Ganesh M, together with OpenSelection (unmodified) and part of KeyboardShortcuts. Their license texts and notices are in Packages/TextActionsKit/Legal and ship inside the app. PepBox's changes to DropClip are listed in Packages/TextActionsKit/PEPBOX_CHANGES.md.
